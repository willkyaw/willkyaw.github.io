Privacy Policy Terms of Use

My Poker Girlfriend, also called Izumi Poker, combines poker drills, quizzes and reference tools with a fictional companion. Feedback uses reference ranges and approximate training models. Postflop feedback and optional AI responses are educational approximations, not exact solver results, and may be wrong. There are no real-money wagers, cash prizes or cash-outs.

For help, bug reports, billing questions or privacy requests, email tinalee1115@gmail.com. Include your device model, app version and what happened. Do not send passwords, payment details or API keys.

Common questions

Where is my progress? Your profile and training progress are saved on your device. The app does not sync them between devices.

How do I restore Premium? Open the Premium screen from Account and choose Restore Purchases using the Apple Account that bought the subscription.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription? Use your App Store account's Subscriptions settings. Deleting the app or local data does not cancel a subscription.

Can I train offline? Core drills, quizzes and bundled voice lines work offline. Optional AI coaching and generated speech require internet access and permission to share the relevant content with the providers described in our privacy policy.

How do I stop AI sharing? Turn it off in Account → AI privacy.
