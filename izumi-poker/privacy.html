Privacy Policy

My Poker Girlfriend / Izumi Poker

Home and SupportTerms of Use

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Your profile and training progress are saved on your device. No account is required. Optional AI features send the content needed for a response to our service providers. This policy explains that processing and the identifiers we retain to enforce access and usage limits.

1. Contact

For support, privacy questions or deletion requests, email tinalee1115@gmail.com. This policy covers My Poker Girlfriend, also referred to as Izumi Poker.

2. Data on your device

Display names, nicknames, profile photos, settings, training results, goals, streaks, chat history and imported hand histories are saved locally. Profile photos are not uploaded by the app. We do not provide cloud account backup or synchronisation. You can clear local data in Account settings or remove the app. If you enable your device's backup services, those backups are managed by your device and Apple.

3. Optional AI and generated voice

Before AI use, the app asks permission to share data. Chat, coaching, hand analysis and training plans send your messages, nickname and relevant hand or training context to Anthropic. Chat context can include recent messages and conversation summaries. Generated voice sends the text to be spoken to ElevenLabs. Bundled voice recordings and offline drills do not require these services.

Premium and free-sample AI requests pass through our Cloudflare relay. Our application code does not write prompts or responses to a database. This does not mean providers never retain content: each provider may retain or process it under its applicable terms, privacy policy, account settings and security obligations. Please avoid sending information you do not want those providers to receive.

If you supply your own Anthropic or ElevenLabs API key, requests go directly to that provider and usage is billed to your provider account. Saved keys use the device Keychain; session-only keys remain in memory. We do not receive your API keys.

4. Access, purchases and usage counters

  • Premium requests include an Apple-signed transaction used to verify your subscription. Daily usage counters are associated with the original transaction identifier and expire after approximately 48 hours. Verified transaction information may also be cached in server memory for up to six hours, subject to earlier subscription expiry.
  • Free AI samples use a device identifier to enforce a lifetime allowance. The identifier and count have no automatic expiry. A daily counter keyed to the connection's IP address is retained for approximately 48 hours to prevent abuse. We do not use IP addresses to determine your location.
  • Identifiers and counters are pseudonymous, not anonymous: they can distinguish a device or purchase. They are used for app functionality, access control and abuse prevention.
  • Apple handles purchases and payment details. The app and relay do not receive your payment card or bank information. Deleting app data does not cancel a subscription; manage subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

5. Advertising, analytics and diagnostics

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising tracking. The app contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDK. App-generated diagnostic reports stay on the device unless you choose to share them. Apple and hosting providers may process operational data under their own policies. Information you email to support is used to answer and resolve your request.

6. Your choices

Turn off AI sharing in Account → AI privacy to prevent future AI requests. Requests already sent cannot be recalled. You can continue offline training. Clear local data in Account settings to remove your local profile, progress and chats. To request deletion of retained relay identifiers or support correspondence, contact us; we may ask for information needed to identify the relevant record. Clearing the app does not by itself delete server-side free-sample counters. Provider-retained content is governed by the providers' policies and deletion procedures.

7. Age and purpose

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over. It is an educational poker simulation with no real-money wagering, cash prizes or cash-out functionality.

8. Changes

We will update this page when our data practices change. The date above identifies the current version.
