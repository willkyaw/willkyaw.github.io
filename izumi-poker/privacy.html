Privacy Policy

Izumi Poker

Home Terms of Use

Last updated: July 7, 2026

The short version: Izumi Poker stores your training data on your device and nowhere else. We do not have user accounts on our own servers and we cannot see your data. When you use Premium AI features, the text you send Izumi is relayed to the AI provider to generate the reply — it is not stored, and it is never tied to your name or email.

1. Who we are

Izumi Poker ("the app", "we") is a poker training application. For any privacy question, contact tinalee1115@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The app keeps everything it needs locally on your device, including:

  • Your display name and the nickname you ask Izumi to call you
  • Training progress: levels, scores, streaks, quest and drill history
  • Settings: language, volume, animation speed, companion preferences
  • Chat history with Izumi
  • Hand-history files you choose to analyze (parsed on your device)

This data never leaves your device unless you use an optional AI feature described below. Deleting the app (or using "Clear all data" in Account settings) permanently erases it. We have no copy.

3. Sign-in, tracking and ads

  • The app works fully as a guest — no sign-up is required. You may optionally use Sign in with Apple to label your progress; the resulting identifier and (if you share it) email address are stored only on your device, never on our servers.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.

4. AI features

Izumi's AI coaching, chat and voice are powered by third-party AI services. They can run in two ways:

  • Premium subscription (default). When you use an AI feature as a Premium subscriber, the text you send — and relevant training context such as the hand being discussed or your category statistics — is sent from your device through our relay server to the AI provider (Anthropic Claude for coaching and chat, ElevenLabs for Izumi's voice) to generate the reply or audio. Our relay does not store your messages or the responses, and it never receives your name, email, or Apple ID. To verify your subscription and prevent abuse it processes your Apple-signed purchase receipt and keeps a daily usage counter keyed to an anonymous App Store transaction number, which expires within 48 hours.
  • Your own API keys (optional). You may instead enter your own Anthropic or ElevenLabs API key in Account settings. Then calls go directly from your device to the provider — bypassing our relay entirely — and are billed to your own provider account. Your keys are stored only on your device; we never see them.

5. Hand-history analysis

If you upload a hand-history export for leak analysis, the file is parsed on your device. Nothing is transmitted until you press Analyze — in which case a summary of the parsed hands is sent to Anthropic (via our relay, or directly with your own key) as described in section 4.

6. Children

The app is a simulated-gambling training tool and is not directed at children. It is intended for users aged 18 and over (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction).

7. Your rights

Because your data lives only on your device, you exercise your rights directly: view it in the app, and erase it at any time via Account → "Clear all data" or by deleting the app. If you have questions about privacy — including rights under GDPR, CCPA, or Japan's APPI — contact us at the address above.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. If a future version of the app ever introduces accounts or server features, this policy will be revised before those features ship.
