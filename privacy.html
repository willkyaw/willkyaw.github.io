Privacy Policy

Izumi Poker

Home Terms of Use

Last updated: July 3, 2026

The short version: Izumi Poker stores your training data on your device and nowhere else. We do not run servers, we do not have user accounts, and we cannot see your data. The only time anything leaves your device is if you choose to connect your own AI or voice API key — and then it goes directly to that provider, never to us.

1. Who we are

Izumi Poker ("the app", "we") is a poker training application. For any privacy question, contact tinalee1115@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The app keeps everything it needs locally on your device, including:

  • Your display name and the nickname you ask Izumi to call you
  • Training progress: levels, scores, streaks, quest and drill history
  • Settings: language, volume, animation speed, companion preferences
  • Chat history with Izumi
  • Hand-history files you choose to analyze (parsed on your device)

This data never leaves your device unless you use an optional AI feature described below. Deleting the app (or using "Clear all data" in Account settings) permanently erases it. We have no copy.

3. No accounts, no tracking, no ads

  • The app has no sign-up, login, or user accounts.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share personal information.

4. Optional AI features (your own API keys)

Some features are powered by third-party AI services that you may connect by entering your own API key in Account settings. These are entirely optional — the app works fully without them.

  • AI coaching & chat (Anthropic Claude). If you add an Anthropic API key, the text you send to the coach or chat — and relevant training context such as the hand being discussed or your category statistics — is sent directly from your device to Anthropic's API to generate a reply. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • Voice (ElevenLabs). If you add an ElevenLabs API key, Izumi's reaction and chat lines are sent directly from your device to ElevenLabs to generate audio. See ElevenLabs' Privacy Policy.

Your API keys are stored only on your device. Calls go directly from your device to the provider — there is no intermediary server operated by us, and we never see your keys, your messages, or the responses. Usage of these services is billed to your own provider account under that provider's terms.

5. Hand-history analysis

If you upload a hand-history export for leak analysis, the file is parsed on your device. Nothing is transmitted unless you have connected an Anthropic API key and press Analyze — in which case a summary of the parsed hands is sent to Anthropic as described in section 4.

6. Children

The app is a simulated-gambling training tool and is not directed at children. It is intended for users aged 18 and over (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction).

7. Your rights

Because your data lives only on your device, you exercise your rights directly: view it in the app, and erase it at any time via Account → "Clear all data" or by deleting the app. If you have questions about privacy — including rights under GDPR, CCPA, or Japan's APPI — contact us at the address above.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. If a future version of the app ever introduces accounts or server features, this policy will be revised before those features ship.
